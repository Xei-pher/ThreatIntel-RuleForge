# Code of Conduct

Be professional and constructive. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is expected; personal attacks are not. Critique the code, evidence, or design decision rather than the person.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior repeatedly harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub rather than escalating them in public threads.
